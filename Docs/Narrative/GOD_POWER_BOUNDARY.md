# DARKWELL — God Power Boundary

Status: canon narrative rule supplement.

This document defines the currently approved boundary between what the unnamed god can do in ordinary reality and what it can do after a settlement has been collected into the otherworld.

## 1. Ordinary reality: influence without full blessing

Before a settlement is collected, the god does not freely manifest as an overt supernatural ruler and does not grant the same controlled blessings seen in collected worlds.

Its practical reach is indirect.

The god can work through:

- temptation;
- suggestion;
- dreams and visions;
- obsession;
- compulsions;
- fear;
- promises;
- religious interpretation;
- corrupted human decision-making.

Humans remain the immediate agents of disaster, ritual, betrayal, and violence.

This rule is important because it keeps the god oppressive without turning ordinary reality into a place where it can simply solve every problem by direct magic.

## 2. Traces can still be dangerous

Objects, remains, structures, substances, symbols, biological material, spatial scars, or other traces connected to the god / collection can produce abnormal effects when discovered in ordinary reality.

Such contact phenomena are not equivalent to deliberate blessing.

For example, in 病村 miners can mutate after uncovering a trace in the mine even though the god cannot yet bestow controlled god-gifts on villagers.

The exact mechanics of such traces remain world-specific and should not be generalized further without approval.

## 3. Collection changes the rules

Once a settlement performs the protection / summoning rite and is removed into the god's extradimensional collection, the god's authority becomes direct.

Inside collected worlds it can:

- take sacrificial parts from people;
- alter bodies and senses;
- remove memories, emotions, identities, relationships, or other human qualities;
- grant abnormal abilities;
- maintain, distort, and exploit the sacrificial cycle;
- apply malicious fulfillment to personal wishes;
- reshape inhabitants in ways that ordinary reality did not permit.

This is the proper domain of "blessings."

## 4. Design consequence

The power boundary creates two different horror modes:

**Before collection:**

> The god persuades people to damn themselves.

**After collection:**

> The god owns the rules of the place they entered.

The transition between these modes should be one of the reasons the protection ritual is so dangerous. A community that believes it is asking a distant supernatural power for help is actually consenting to enter the space where that power can finally act on them directly.

## 5. Story guardrail

Future narrative work must not casually give the unnamed god unrestricted direct miracle powers in ordinary reality.

If an event before collection appears physically supernatural, it should be explained through one of the following unless a later canon decision changes this rule:

- dangerous contact with a trace linked to the otherworld;
- a human acting under influence;
- ambiguity about what actually happened;
- a phenomenon already crossing the boundary from a collected space.

Full malicious blessings, bodily prices, and controlled god-gifts belong to the collected otherworld.
