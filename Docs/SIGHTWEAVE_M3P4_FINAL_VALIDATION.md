## 4. Algorithm selection and rejected routes

The selected implementation is a dirty-tile-derived PF_G8 VisualFeather atlas generated by a bounded jump-flood distance transform in world texel space. Seed and final passes resolve hard samples through the logical page table; physical slot adjacency never defines world adjacency.

Screen-space blur was rejected for leakage, depth/resolution dependence, and TAA/camera instability. Full-screen world multi-tap was rejected for per-pixel page-table cost. Separable blur/erosion was rejected for axis-biased corners and radius-scaled passes. A full-atlas transform was rejected because unchanged scopes must not rebuild. SceneCapture/GBuffer-color-derived approaches were rejected because they form a second authority and violate packaging scope.

## 5. Hard/Visual isolation

```text
FinalPresentationWeight = HardLivePointSample * VisualFeatherWeight
OutputColor = SceneColor * FinalPresentationWeight
```

HardLive remains binary, point-sampled, and authoritative. VisualFeather is a separately named presentation resource, never writes into HardLive, and has no gameplay, AI, HUD, interaction, lock-on, memory, Last-Seen, save, or DARKWELL dependency. The production shader evaluates the hard point gate before sampling the continuous feather weight.

## 6. World-space width definition

Width is configured in centimeters, defaults to 50 cm for development, and is clamped to 0..100 cm. Standard precision is 2.5 cm/texel, so 50 cm and 100 cm map to 20 and 40 texels. The transform has a fixed maximum halo of 40 texels. The first JFA step is the next power of two for the active radius (32 or 64), keeping work bounded without changing world width. Camera position, rotation, FOV/OrthoWidth, viewport size, resolution scale, and temporal jitter do not dirty or resize the world atlas.

## 7. Resource and dirty-update strategy

Each Feather page mirrors a hard page's logical-to-physical residency address but is a separate PF_G8 texture. Two reusable 328x328 PF_G32R32F scratch textures service tiles serially. Hard dirty and removed logical tiles expand by the bounded width neighborhood; every neighbor is resolved through the page table. Slots are cleared before derive/reuse. A no-change frame calls the production preparation path but performs zero Mask or Feather dispatch and no upload/allocation/resource generation.

## 8. Fail closed

Bindings include world, owner, floor, precision, canonical profiles, packet/registry/snapshot/presentation revisions, hard resource/residency generation, Feather resource generation, Feather applied revision, and Feather settings revision. Missing shader/format/resource, mismatched provenance, incomplete dirty work, missing logical neighbor, stale command, eviction, teardown, or unavailable scope rejects the binding and produces black. Old Feather, another world/scope, or full Scene Color is never used as fallback.
