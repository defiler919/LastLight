# DARKWELL design pillars

## Core fantasy

The player survives oppressive darkness by balancing two hands:

- Left hand: a one-handed, sawed-off double-barrel shotgun fired with the left mouse button.
- Right hand: a torch, lantern, or empty hand. The torch uses tap swings and a held close-range boundary that prevents enemies from closing through its aimed area. The lantern uses passive light, a held focus beam that gradually fills an enemy stun meter, and a tap flash for immediate control windows.

## Survival pressure

- Darkness limits reliable information instead of merely darkening the image.
- Unexplored space is completely unknown; explored space becomes stale memory; only current sight is authoritative.
- Walls and closed passages interrupt vision. HUD threat information and interactions must never reveal live state outside current sight.
- Walking permits independent mouse aiming at a limited turn rate; sprinting sacrifices rear awareness by forcing sight toward travel.
- Light reveals threats but can also reveal the player.
- The shotgun is decisive, loud, and constrained to two immediate shots.
- Reloading needs the right hand, forcing the player to put down or throw a burning torch.
- Ammunition is scarce and shotgun shells are crafted at a workbench.
- Encounters must allow avoidance, escape, or fire-based control so limited ammunition does not create a progression dead end.
- Torch heat and lantern fuel make light control a timed decision rather than permanent crowd suppression.

## Enemy roles

- Stalker: fast baseline hunter with 120 health and 16-damage attacks. A held torch enforces the full stand-off boundary, making it the readable target for close group control.
- Warden: slow armored fuse guard with 260 health and 28-damage attacks. It pushes through the outer torch field and stops only at 58% of the normal boundary, so torch hold buys safety but not comfortable space.
- Lantern focus does not alter either enemy before its individual meter fills. A full meter always creates a five-second stun; the Warden's higher threat and durability make that aimed control window especially valuable.

## First prototype question

Does the interaction between gun, torch, darkness, sound, and reloading create a tense and readable five-minute encounter before final art exists?
